## Question

Which six to seven lenses does run 2 generate through, and what are the two
distinct generator angles within each lens? The charter fixes the frame: the two
binding constraints (identification/empirics, welfare model) remain primary, plus
one explicitly open lens. To decide with Jacopo: whether the first run's five
lenses (identification and causal design; welfare model and theory; data-asset
exploitation; literature import; hostile referee) survive as-is, get reworked, or
are partly replaced; what the sixth/seventh lenses are; how each lens splits into
two independent generator angles so paired generators do not converge on the same
ideas; and how the open lens is worded so it genuinely escapes the constraints
framing without producing unusable blue-sky material.

## Resolution (2026-07-09)

Settled in a live grilling session with Jacopo; every decision below was put to him
individually and confirmed. The run-1 ledger informed the recommendations: the
identification and data-asset lenses produced advancers, both solo literature-import
ideas were killed at Checkpoint 2, and hostile referee never produced a standalone
survivor.

1. **Posture on the run-1 lenses.** Identification and causal design, welfare model
   and theory, and data-asset exploitation survive. Literature import survives but is
   reworked: it imports framings and welfare-measurement strategies rather than raw
   techniques, and every candidate must state why the import is easier here than in
   the source paper. Hostile referee is demoted from a lens to a generator angle
   inside the identification lens.

2. **The lens set is seven lenses:** (1) identification and causal design,
   (2) welfare model and theory, (3) data-asset exploitation, (4) literature import
   (reworked), (5) regulation and firm behaviour — the supply side run 1 never
   touched: enforcement, fines, Ofwat price reviews, the incentives that generate
   spills, and the regulatory counterfactual the damage estimates feed,
   (6) distributional incidence — who bears the spill burden and who counterfactual
   policies help, across income, tenure, demographics, and place, and (7) the open
   lens. Seven rather than six because two generators per lens at four to six ideas
   each puts the first round at 56–84 raw ideas, matching the charter's 60–80 target,
   and the budget ticket can trim a lens later if costs bite, whereas a generation
   gap cannot be patched after the run.

3. **Generator angles for the six named lenses.** Paired generators run blind to each
   other and must differ in method of attack: identification splits into constructive
   design-finding versus hostile referee (rejection letter converted into pre-empting
   designs); welfare splits into top-down model building versus bottom-up
   measurement-first bounding; data-asset splits into depth in existing assets
   (including what the works-grain merge newly permits) versus new acquisitions;
   literature import splits into within-field (recent top-5 environmental and urban)
   versus adjacent-field (public finance, IO, health, spatial); regulation splits into
   empirical policy variation versus incentive modelling; distributional incidence
   splits into incidence measurement versus policy incidence (damage-weighted versus
   equity-weighted enforcement).

4. **The open lens escapes the constraints framing structurally, not rhetorically.**
   Angle (a), "the different paper", receives only the data-asset inventory — not the
   draft's argument, designs, or the binding-constraints framing — and asks what the
   best paper hiding in these data is. Angle (b), "the reframe", receives the full
   brief and asks what framing, headline claim, or title-level recasting makes the
   existing results a general-interest top-5 paper. Both prompts carry a shared
   feasibility tether: every idea must name the specific data it runs on (existing
   assets or at most one realistic acquisition) and state the headline result a top-5
   editor would care about; no field work, no experiments, no implausible data.

Consequence for the context pack: angle (a) of the open lens requires a
framing-free data-asset inventory as a separate context-pack artifact, prepared so it
can be handed over without leaking the draft's argument. This is noted in the map's
Not yet specified section for the spec-assembly ticket to pick up.
